Go-to-market plan

Selling The Oval with Angela Webb

The Oval is at concept stage and pre-consent, so the first job of marketing is to build a register of qualified buyers who see plans and pricing first. With only 16 homes, the goal is a short, high-quality register rather than volume. Figures marked "estimate" are starting assumptions to test, not results.

Perfect customer

Primary · detached homes

Growing families upsizing

Couples aged 30 to 45 with two or more children aged 3 to 14, often in an older three-bedroom home or rental in Sockburn, Upper Riccarton, Hornby, Hei Hei, Broomfield or Wigram. Includes many Indian, Filipino and Chinese families who already live in the west of Christchurch.

  • Trigger: a child starting school, a new baby, or a rental that's too small or too cold.
  • Wants: a safe street for bikes, schools within walking distance, a garage and a garden.
  • Objections: buying off the plans feels risky, and the timing is unknown before consent.

Primary · terrace homes

First home buyers and young couples

Aged 25 to 38, using KiwiSaver and First Home Partner or family help, working in Hornby, Sockburn, Middleton or near the airport.

  • Trigger: rent rising, a deposit finally saved, or a baby on the way.
  • Wants: a new, warm, low-maintenance home with a garage, close to work.
  • Objections: deposit size and finance approval timing.

Secondary

Investors

Local and North Island buyers wanting new-build, low-maintenance rentals near the university, airport and Hornby employment hub. Angela's investment database is the natural first audience.

  • Objections: rental yield and the time until completion.

Secondary

Parents buying near family

Grandparents aged 55 to 70 who want to be close to their grandchildren and their schools, often moving from a larger section nearby.

  • Objections: stairs in a two-storey home, and selling their own home first.

Funnel

Step 1Ad

One clear promise: first access to plans and pricing.

Step 2Landing page

The Oval site, opening on the family street film.

Step 3Register form

Four required fields: name, email, phone and home type.

Step 4Speed to lead

Angela calls or texts within 5 minutes during business hours.

Step 5Nurture

Information pack email straight away, then fortnightly updates on consent, plans and the release date.

Step 6Retarget

Visitors who didn't register see school, agent and early-access ads for 30 days.

Tracking

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API: a "Lead" event on successful form submission, sent from the browser and the server with deduplication.
  • Google Ads conversion tag and GA4: a "generate_lead" event on the same submission, with the home type as a parameter.
  • UTM tags on every ad, so each registration in the CRM shows its platform, angle and ad number.
  • Weekly report: spend, registrations, cost per registration, qualified rate and the best ads.

Traditional media: billboards, print and outdoor

Traditional media builds local awareness that sends people to search for The Oval, so it works best alongside the digital ads above. Every piece carries Angela, one call to action and the site address. The mock-ups below are concepts for discussion, not booked placements, and the web address is a placeholder until the domain is chosen.

Booking a Christchurch billboard

Step 1Brief the suppliers

Ask the main outdoor companies (such as JCDecaux, oOh!media and LUMO Digital Outdoor) for available sites and rates.

Step 2Choose sites

Pick roads western-suburbs families drive daily, such as Main South Road, Riccarton Road, Yaldhurst Road and Blenheim Road.

Step 3Book dates

Digital boards book in 1 to 2 week slots. Printed boards usually run for 4 weeks or more.

Step 4Supply artwork

Use the supplier's exact size and resolution. Keep it to 7 words or fewer plus Angela and the web address.

Step 5Go live and track

Watch direct visits and branded searches for "The Oval Sockburn" during the run.

Rules for every ad

  • Label renders as artist's impressions, concept only and not consented, which keeps the ads in line with the Fair Trading Act.
  • Don't quote prices, completion dates or bedroom counts that aren't confirmed.
  • School walk times are approximate, and ads must not promise school zoning.
  • Include Bayleys branding and the licence line wherever the format has room.
