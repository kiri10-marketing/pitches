Launch plan

Getting BuildMap in front of the people who make property happen

One campaign per audience, so each gets its own budget and its own landing page. Every ad leads to the free first Lite report, because it costs the person nothing to try and it shows the product better than anything we can say in an ad. Figures marked "estimate" are starting points to replace with real results after the first fortnight.

Audience 1

Homeowners and landowners

People in Christchurch, Selwyn and Waimakariri on bigger sections, often 45 to 70, who've wondered whether their back lawn could be a second home or a few townhouses.

  • Lands on: /landowners
  • Hook: find out what your section could become, first report free.
  • Worry to answer: "It'll be complicated and expensive to find out." The Lite report is free and in plain words.

Audience 2

Architects and designers

Small to mid-size Christchurch practices doing residential and multi-unit work, who lose hours on early planning homework and on clients whose sites can't carry the brief.

  • Lands on: /builders for now (the closest page: reports in your own branding). A dedicated architects page would lift conversion.
  • Hook: start sketching from what the site allows, and offer feasibility as your own service.
  • Worry to answer: "Can I trust the rules?" Planning oversight from LG Consulting.

Audience 3

Real estate agents

Residential and land agents in Canterbury, especially those listing older homes on large or corner sections in medium-density zones.

  • Lands on: /agents (with the commission calculator)
  • Hook: walk into the appraisal with a plan, and pitch the on-sale of every home on the site.
  • Worry to answer: "Will vendors care?" Vendors pick the agent who shows them the most their land can do.

Tracking before launch

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API on the site, with a Lead event when the order form succeeds, sent from the browser and the server and deduplicated. The site doesn't have the Pixel yet, so this is the one job to do before switching ads on.
  • UTM tags are already on every landing link below, so each order shows its audience, angle and size.
  • Weekly check: spend, addresses sent, cost per address, paid upgrades and the best three ads.

Rules for every ad

What every ad must keep to

  • Every ad carries "Concept only. Subject to council consent." We map the route; the council and the market make the calls, so no ad promises a consent, a yield, a price or a sale result.
  • Ads talk about zoning, rules, hazards and planning oversight. They never sell or suggest third-party property data such as sales records or valuations, and none of it is offered for resale.
  • Prices match the site: first Lite report free with an account, technical report $499 within 24 hours, full development plan $2,499 plus GST within 48 hours, full package $4,999 plus GST within 5 working days.
  • Pictures are illustrations made with AI tools, not real projects or real clients. The plans on them are artwork, not BuildMap drawings.
  • Voice: plain, calm and conversational, no exclamation marks, no "revolutionary" or "AI-powered" headlines.
