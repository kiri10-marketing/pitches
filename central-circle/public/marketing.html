Go-to-market plan

Filling a village on Kilmore Street with Circle Living

The Central Circle sells a way of living before it sells a floor plan: your own front door, a shared garden and neighbours you know, in the middle of Christchurch. Cohousing is new to most buyers, so the campaign has two jobs: explain it simply, and grow a list of interested households who come to an Open Hour and join the Founding Circle before pre-sales open. Pricing is still to be released and the concept is not yet designed in detail or consented, so no ad shows a price or a completion date, and every render is labelled as an artist's impression. Figures marked "estimate" are starting assumptions to test.

Perfect customer

Primary

Right-sizers who don't want to be alone

Owners aged 55 to 78 in larger Christchurch homes (Merivale, St Albans, Fendalton, Cashmere, Sumner), with equity and grown children, often living alone or as a couple.

  • Trigger: the house and section are too much, a partner has died, or friends are moving away.
  • Wants: a smaller home of their own, a garden without the upkeep, company when they want it, walkable to town.
  • Objections: "is it a commune?", stairs, body corporate costs, buying before consent.

Primary

Young families who want a village

Couples aged 28 to 45 with young children, renting or in a first home, who want the central city without losing a garden and safe places to play.

  • Trigger: a new baby, starting school, or feeling isolated in a suburb.
  • Wants: a two- or three-bedroom townhouse, other kids nearby, trusted neighbours.
  • Objections: price, timing, school zones, space.

Secondary

Cohousing-curious singles and couples

People aged 30 to 65 who already follow cohousing, intentional community, sustainability or slow-living ideas, including Circle Living's existing followers and waiting lists.

  • Objections: how decisions are made and what is shared; answered by the Open Hour and Kōwhai's track record.

Secondary

Multi-generational families

Grandparents and their adult children who want to live close, each in their own home, around one garden.

  • Objections: securing homes side by side; answered by joining the Founding Circle together.

Tracking

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API: a "Lead" event on successful registration, browser and server, deduplicated.
  • Google Ads conversion tag and GA4: a "generate_lead" event with home of interest and household type as parameters.
  • UTM tags on every ad, so each registration shows its platform, angle and ad number.
  • Weekly report: spend, registrations, cost per registration, Open Hour bookings and the best ads.
